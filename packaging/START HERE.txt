AirMouse - control your mouse with hand gestures
================================================


TO START
--------

  Double-click  AirMouse.exe


  The first time, Windows may show a blue box saying
  "Windows protected your PC".

  That box appears for any program that hasn't paid for a Microsoft
  signing certificate. It is not a virus warning.

      Click "More info", then click "Run anyway".


TO USE IT
---------

  A window opens showing what your webcam sees.

  1. Hold up a flat open palm - like a high five - for about a second.
     A circle locks onto your hand. You now have the cursor.

  2. Move your hand to move the cursor.

  3. Touch your thumb to your index finger to click.

  4. Hold thumb and index together to drag something.

  5. Lower your hand to let go. Raise it again to carry on.

  Press  Q  to quit.
  Press  B  to open settings.
  Press  D  to see live numbers for why a gesture is or isn't firing.

  There is more - scrolling, right click, volume, swipe to go back and
  forward, and a launcher on your other hand. Press B, or read the full
  guide at the link below.


IF IT DOESN'T WORK
------------------

  Nothing happens / it says the camera is busy
      Something else is using your webcam. Close Discord, Zoom, Teams,
      OBS, or any browser tab that has camera access, then start
      AirMouse again. Only one program can use a webcam at a time.

  The cursor is jumpy, or your hand isn't detected
      The room is probably too dark. AirMouse needs reasonable light.

  Windows won't let it see the camera
      Settings > Privacy & security > Camera
      "Let desktop apps access your camera" must be turned On.


WHAT THIS DOES TO YOUR COMPUTER
-------------------------------

  Nothing outside this folder.

  AirMouse carries its own private copy of Python inside the _internal
  folder. It does not install anything, does not change your PATH, and
  does not touch a Python installation you already have.

  Your settings are saved to config.json, right here in this folder.

  To uninstall: delete this folder. That's all of it.
